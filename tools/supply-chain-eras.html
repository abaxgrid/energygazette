Who controlled each link, from wellhead to market, in four eras. In 1970 foreign majors ran the chain and the state collected rent. The 2030 outlook, built on 100-year leases and private marketing, looks structurally closer to 1970 than to 1990.

The arc

Foreign majors built the chain and kept it until nationalization in 1976. PDVSA then owned every link, bought refineries abroad to place its heavy crude, and by 2010 still held the chain while output and maintenance slid. The July 2026 regulation does not mention PDVSA at all.

Sources and method

1970–2010 from the historical record: concession-era company reports, PDVSA annual reports, OPEC statistics and the Hydrocarbons Law and its reforms.
