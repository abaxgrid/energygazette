The living timeline

From Statute to Signature, 2026

Every edition so far hangs off these dates. Tap one to see the instrument, what it changed, what is still open, and where the Gazette covered it.

InstrumentOn-record statementSigningListing decision
29 January 2026 · Instrument

The Hydrocarbons Law Is Reformed

The National Assembly amends the Organic Hydrocarbons Law. Royalties can reach 30%, a new Integrated Hydrocarbons Tax of up to 15% on gross revenue is set project by project, operatorship can be delegated to minority partners, and an economic-financial equilibrium guarantee is written in.

Still openWhether the Integrated Hydrocarbons Tax is deductible for income-tax purposes.
SourceGaceta Oficial N° 6.978 Extraordinario · LOH Arts. 26, 36, 51, 55–57
Read it in Issue No. 2 · The Rate Arrived on 7 July Special · Anatomy of an Oil MoU
3 April 2026 · Instrument

The Fiscal Provisions Take Effect

The reform's fiscal chapter comes into force. The statute now holds ceilings, not rates: royalty and the integrated tax are capped, but the figure for any given project is left to executive discretion.

Still openNo assessable rate for any project yet. Investors can model the ceiling, not the number.
SourceGaceta Oficial N° 6.978 Extraordinario · transitional provisions
Read it in Issue No. 2 · The Rate Arrived on 7 July
29 May 2026 · On-record statement

Chevron Says the Numbers Are Missing

On Bloomberg TV, Chevron's chief executive says the reform points in the right direction but the fiscal values that decide project economics have not been specified. It is the clearest public statement of what investors were waiting for.

Still openWhich instrument would fill the gap, and when.
SourceBloomberg Television interview, 29 May 2026
Read it in Issue No. 2 · The Rate Arrived on 7 July
7 July 2026 · Instrument

The Rate Arrives

The implementing regulation turns ceilings into an assessable framework: an aggregate-rate schedule (Art. 43), and a 34% income-tax rate with seven-year accelerated depreciation for greenfield projects (Art. 45). Tax resolutions follow in G.O. N° 43.410. Largely unreported at the time, this is the hinge between May's complaint and September's signatures.

Still openNo project-specific rate determination had been published in the Gaceta as of Issue No. 2.
SourceDecreto N° 5.381, Gaceta Oficial N° 7.052 Extraordinario · G.O. N° 43.410
Read it in Issue No. 2 · The Rate Arrived on 7 July Issue No. 3 · A Gold Rush, Read Twice Run the numbers · Fiscal take calculator
September 2026 · Signings

The Signatures Follow

Chevron sets out a $7 billion, five-year plan across its joint ventures, a scaling-up rather than a return. Continental Resources signs an MoU with PDVSA for the Ayacucho 2 block on 16 September, and TotalEnergies signs one on 19 September.

Still openThe signed MoU texts are not public. Every clause that matters still depends on an OFAC licence.
SourceCompany announcements · CNBC interview, Caracas, 2 September 2026
Read it in Issue No. 1 · Chevron Bets Bigger Special · Anatomy of an Oil MoU Briefing · Who's Operating in Venezuela
9 October 2026 · Listing decision

Panama Comes Off the EU List

The EU removes Panama from its list of non-cooperative tax jurisdictions, tied to the new economic-substance law and its implementing decree. Panama becomes a defensible platform for Venezuela-facing work, but only for structures with real substance.

Still openVenezuela's own EU and FATF standing, being verified for the next brief.
SourceCouncil of the EU, list of non-cooperative jurisdictions · Panama Law 526
